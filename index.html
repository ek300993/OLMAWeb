Still curious? Say hello ↗

What is OLMA?

OLMA is a photo price comparison app for iPhone and iPad. Photograph a product or price tag to find current retailer prices and get context on whether the price is a good deal.

How can I compare prices from a photo?

Open OLMA and photograph the product or price tag. The app identifies the product and searches for prices. Check that the model, size, and condition match before using the comparison to decide.

Is OLMA free to use?

OLMA includes 5 free price scans per month without an account. Optional Pro subscriptions unlock unlimited scans and faster results. Check the App Store purchase screen for the price and terms in your region.

Can I compare prices while shopping abroad?

Yes. OLMA compares prices locally and in your home market, with currency conversion for context. Check your final payment cost and any applicable fees before buying.

Does OLMA guarantee the lowest price?

No. Product matches, available offers, and prices can change. Confirm the exact item, stock, shipping, taxes, and return terms with the retailer. A price verdict helps you investigate a deal; it does not guarantee savings.

Do I need a barcode to check a price?

No. OLMA can start a comparison from a photo of the product or price tag. Clear photos that show the brand, model, and size help you check that the result matches the item.
