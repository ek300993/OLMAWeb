How to save money shopping: a before-you-buy checklist

By OLMA · Shopping guides from the makers of the app

The quick answer

To save money shopping, decide what you need before looking at deals, compare the total cost of equivalent products, and buy only the quantity you will use. A price comparison helps you choose between purchases; skipping an unnecessary purchase keeps the whole amount unspent.

1. Set the purchase and your target price first

Write down the problem you need to solve: replacing broken headphones, buying a winter coat, or restocking a household product. Then choose the features that matter and the most you are comfortable spending.

That short note gives you a test for tempting offers. If a discounted item does not solve the original problem, it is a new purchase to consider separately. You do not need to turn every sale into a buying decision.

2. Compare equivalent products and quantities

For an exact product, match the model and condition. For consumables you are happy to substitute, compare the cost per unit as well as the pack price.

Imagine a 500 mL bottle costs $8 and a 750 mL bottle of the same product costs $10. The first is $1.60 per 100 mL; the second is about $1.33 per 100 mL. The larger bottle has a lower unit price, but still requires $2 more at checkout. These are example prices.

The larger size only helps if you will use the extra amount and have room to store it. A low unit price is not useful when the surplus goes unused. For products with different concentrations, compare usable doses rather than bottle size alone.

3. Test the conditions of the deal

A free-shipping threshold can turn a small order into a bigger one. If shipping is $6 and you add an unnecessary $15 item to avoid it, you spend $9 more overall. Add something only when you already intended to buy it and the combined order makes sense.

Apply the same logic to multi-buy offers and memberships. Calculate the cost for the amount you need today. If a membership is required, include its cost when deciding whether that particular purchase saves money.

4. Make a quick check before checkout

  • Is this the item or specification I planned to buy?
  • Have I checked another available offer for the same product?
  • Does my comparison include delivery and other checkout costs?
  • Will I use everything in this pack or bundle?
  • Would I still want it without the sale sign?

For an unplanned purchase, step away long enough to check those answers. You can make a note of the product and price without committing to it.
